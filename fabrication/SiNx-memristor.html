Process Flow: Crossbar TiN / SiNx / TiN Memristive Devices

  • Substrate: 100 mm or 150 mm p-type Si (100) prime wafer with 200–500 nm thermally grown wet thermal SiO2.
  • Active Area: Metal-Insulator-Metal (MIM) junction defined by bottom electrode (BE) and top electrode (TE) overlap.
  • Switching Layer: 5 nm stoichiometric/sub-stoichiometric PECVD silicon nitride (SiNx).

Process Overview & Mask Levels

Mask Level Layer Name Polarity Description
Mask 01 Bottom Electrode (BE) Dark Field / Inverted Defines bottom TiN wire and probe pads (Lift-off).
Mask 02 Contact Via (BE Opening) Clear Field Opens bottom TiN pad window through blanket SiNx.
Mask 03 Top Electrode (TE) Dark Field / Inverted Defines top TiN lines crossing BE to form junctions (Lift-off).

Module 1: Substrate Preparation & Pre-Clean

Step 1.1: Solvent Degrease

  • Objective: Eliminate organic residues, particles, and hydrocarbons from wafer surface.
  • Equipment / Tool: Wet Chemical Bench (Solvent Hood) with ultrasonic / megasonic bath.
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters:
    • Ultrasonic bath in Acetone: 5 min at 45 °C (or ambient with 40 kHz sonication).
    • Ultrasonic bath in Isopropanol (IPA): 5 min at ambient.
    • Cascade Deionized (DI) Water Rinse: Resistivity > 18.2 MΩ·cm until dump rinse cycle completes.
    • Blow dry under filtered high-purity N2 gun.

Step 1.2: Piranha / RCA Clean (Optional / Baseline Clean)

  • Objective: Complete organic stripping and particulate removal on thermal oxide.
  • Equipment / Tool: Acid Bench with quartz bath.
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters:
    • H2SO4 (96%) : H2O2 (30%) in 3:1 volumetric ratio.
    • Temperature: 80–100 °C (self-heating), immersion for 10 min.
    • Cascade DI water rinse until bath resistivity reaches > 15 MΩ·cm.
    • Spin-Rinse-Dryer (SRD) run.

Step 1.3: Dehydration Bake

  • Objective: Desorb surface moisture to promote photoresist and primer adhesion.
  • Equipment / Tool: Cleanroom Contact Hotplate or Convection Vacuum Oven.
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters:
    • Hotplate: 150 °C for 5 min (or vacuum oven at 120 °C for 30 min).
    • Cool down to room temperature on a cold aluminium chill plate for 60 s.

Module 2: Lithography 1 – Bottom Electrode (BE) Lift-Off Patterning

Step 2.1: Surface Priming (Vapour Prime)

  • Objective: Improve interfacial adhesion and avoid resist lift during aqueous development.
  • Equipment / Tool: Dedicated HMDS Priming Oven (e.g., YES-310TA) or manual spin chuck.
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters:
    • HMDS gas phase deposition at 150 °C for 5 min (or spin HDMS primer at 4000 rpm for 30 s, bake at 110 °C for 60 s).

Step 2.2: Resist Coating (AZ 2070 Negative Photoresist)

  • Objective: Dispense negative-tone crosslinking resist yielding a re-entrant (negative) undercut profile for clean lift-off.
  • Equipment / Tool: Automated Spin Coater (e.g., Brewer Science Cee 200X / Süss Delta 80).
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters:
    • Dynamic dispense: ~3–4 mL of AZ 2070.
    • Spread cycle: 500 rpm for 5 s (ramp: 500 rpm/s).
    • Spin cycle: 3000–4000 rpm for 45 s (ramp: 3000 rpm/s) to target film thickness ~6.5–7.0 µm.
    • Dynamic backside and edge bead removal (EBR) with PGMEA / AZ EBR solvent: 10 s.

Step 2.3: Soft Bake

  • Objective: Evaporate casting solvent (PGMEA) to stabilise resist film without initiating thermal crosslinking.
  • Equipment / Tool: Precision Vacuum Contact Hotplate.
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters:
    • Temperature: 110 °C (± 0.5 °C).
    • Duration: 60 s (contact mode).
    • Chill plate: 20 °C for 30 s.

Step 2.4: Photolithographic Exposure (Mask 01)

  • Objective: Expose BE track and contact geometry to activate photoacid generator (PAG).
  • Equipment / Tool: Mask Aligner (e.g., Süss MicroTec MA6 / BA6) or DUV/i-line Stepper.
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters:
    • Light Source: Mercury arc lamp, i-line filtered (λ = 365 nm).
    • Contact Mode: Vacuum contact mode.
    • Dose: 140–180 mJ/cm² (calibrated to undercut target).

Step 2.5: Post-Exposure Bake (PEB)

  • Objective: Catalyse acid-catalysed crosslinking in UV-exposed areas (insolubilising them).
  • Equipment / Tool: Precision Vacuum Contact Hotplate.
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters:
    • Temperature: 110 °C.
    • Duration: 60 s.
    • Chill plate: 20 °C for 30 s.

Step 2.6: Development

  • Objective: Selectively dissolve unexposed (uncrosslinked) resist to produce retrograde sidewall undercuts.
  • Equipment / Tool: Wet Bench / Developer Puddle Cup.
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters:
    • Developer: Metal-Ion-Free developer (TMAH-based, e.g., AZ 726 MIF or AZ 300 MIF, 2.38% normality).
    • Mode: Double puddle or spray/immersion with mild agitation: 60–75 s at 21 °C.
    • Rinse: Quench immediately in overflow DI water bath for 60 s.
    • Dry: High-purity N2 blow-dry.
    • Metrology: Optical microscope check / SEM cross-section inspection for lift-off overhang profiles.

Step 2.7: Descum / Surface Activation (RIE)

  • Objective: Clear residual resist scum from open SiO2 areas to ensure reproducible metal adhesion and low contact resistance.
  • Equipment / Tool: Reactive Ion Etcher (RIE) or Downstream Asher (e.g., Oxford Plasmalab 80 Plus / March CS1701).
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters:
    • Gas: O2 = 30 sccm (optional: Ar = 5 sccm).
    • Chamber Pressure: 50–100 mTorr.
    • RF Power: 50 W.
    • Etch Time: 30–45 s.

Module 3: Bottom Electrode Metallisation & Lift-Off

Step 3.1: In-situ Pre-Clean

  • Objective: Remove adsorbed ambient species and native oxide prior to deposition.
  • Equipment / Tool: Sputter Cluster Tool (in-situ load lock / etch chamber).
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters:
    • Ar ion milling / reverse bias RF sputter clean: 50 W, 5 mTorr Ar, 60 s.

Step 3.2: Bottom TiN Sputtering (60 nm)

  • Objective: Deposit dense, smooth, metallic conductive bottom electrode layer.
  • Equipment / Tool: DC Magnetron Sputter System (e.g., AJA Orion, Kurt J. Lesker LAB 18, or Singulus).
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters:
    • Target: Pure Titanium (99.995%) reactive sputtering (or compound TiN target with RF).
    • Base Pressure: < 5 × 10⁻⁷ Torr.
    • Process Pressure: 3.0 mTorr.
    • Working Gases: Ar = 20 sccm, N2 = 4–6 sccm (poisoned / transition mode tuned for stoichiometry).
    • DC Sputter Power: 250–300 W.
    • Substrate Temperature: Ambient to 150 °C.
    • Substrate Rotation: 20 rpm (ensures uniform thickness across wafer).
    • Deposition Rate: ~0.15 nm/s → Total time: ~400 s to reach 60 nm TiN.

Step 3.3: Bottom Metal Lift-Off

  • Objective: Strip AZ 2070 and discard unwanted TiN film atop resist, retaining only bottom tracks.
  • Equipment / Tool: Solvent Lift-Off Bench with heated tank and ultrasonic transducer.
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters:
    • Stripper: NMP (N-Methyl-2-pyrrolidone) or dedicated lift-off solvent (Microposit 1165 / TechniStrip NI555).
    • Temperature: 70–80 °C.
    • Soak time: 30–60 min static soak, followed by low-power ultrasonic agitation (20–40 kHz) for 2–5 min to detach metal flakes.
    • High-pressure solvent spray wand (IPA) to wash away suspended TiN ribbons.
    • Intermediate IPA bath rinse (prevent redeposition) → DI water cascade rinse.
    • Spin dry under N2.
    • Metrology: Profilometry / AFM to verify BE step height (60 nm) and absence of burrs/rabbit-ears.

Module 4: Functional Switching Layer Deposition (5 nm Blanket SiNx)

Step 4.1: Pre-Deposition Surface Preparation

  • Objective: Clean BE surface prior to critical dielectric interface growth.
  • Equipment / Tool: Solvent rinse (IPA/DI water), followed by mild in-situ H2/Ar or N2 plasma passivate (removes superficial Ti-oxide).

Step 4.2: Blanket SiNx Deposition (5 nm)

  • Objective: Deposit high-precision, low-defect, sub-stoichiometric/stoichiometric silicon nitride switching medium.
  • Equipment / Tool: Plasma Enhanced Chemical Vapour Deposition (PECVD) system (e.g., Oxford Instruments PlasmaPro 100 / STS Multiplex).
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters:
    • Temperature: 250–300 °C (low thermal budget to avoid TiN stress relaxation/oxidation).
    • Precursor Gases:
      • 2% or 5% Silane in He/N2 balance (SiH4): 10–20 sccm
      • Ammonia (NH3): 5–15 sccm
      • Nitrogen (N2): 200–400 sccm
    • Chamber Pressure: 600–1000 mTorr.
    • RF Power (High Frequency, 13.56 MHz): 20–40 W (low-power regime to minimize ion bombardment damage).
    • Calibrated Deposition Rate: ~0.08–0.12 nm/s (strictly calibrated using spectroscopic ellipsometry dummy runs).
    • Deposition Time: ~45–60 s to target 5.0 nm (± 0.2 nm).
    • Metrology: In-situ or post Spectroscopic Ellipsometry (measure film thickness \(t\) and refractive index \(n \approx 1.85\text{--}2.05\) at 633 nm).

Module 5: Lithography 2 & RIE – Bottom Contact Via Opening

Step 5.1: Resist Coating (Positive Tone or AZ 2070 used in direct mode)

  • Objective: Create etch mask protecting the active junction while exposing the BE landing pad area.
  • Equipment / Tool: Automated Spin Coater.
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters:
    • Spin standard positive resist (e.g., AZ 1512 or Megaposit SPR 220) at 3000 rpm (thickness ~1.2 µm).
    • Soft bake: 100 °C for 60 s.

Step 5.2: Photolithographic Alignment & Exposure (Mask 02)

  • Objective: Align via mask relative to underlying BE fiducial marks.
  • Equipment / Tool: Mask Aligner (Süss MA6).
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters:
    • Layer-to-layer optical alignment (tolerance < 0.5 µm).
    • Exposure Dose: 80–120 mJ/cm² (calibrated for thin positive resist).
    • Development: AZ 726 MIF for 45 s, DI cascade rinse, N2 blow-dry.
    • Inspection: Verify via opening directly over BE pad extension.

Step 5.3: Reactive Ion Etching (RIE) of 5 nm SiNx

  • Objective: Anisotropically etch 5 nm SiNx to expose TiN bottom electrode contact pad with high selectivity to TiN.
  • Equipment / Tool: Fluorine RIE / ICP-RIE System (e.g., Oxford Plasmalab 80 Plus).
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters:
    • Etch Gases: CHF3 = 30 sccm, O2 = 2–5 sccm (or CF4/Ar chemistry: 25 sccm CF4 / 10 sccm Ar).
    • Chamber Pressure: 30–40 mTorr.
    • RF Power: 30–50 W (DC bias ~80–120 V).
    • Etch Rate: ~0.4–0.6 nm/s.
    • Process Time: 12–15 s (includes 20% over-etch to clear interfacial layer; TiN acts as an etch stop).

Step 5.4: Resist Strip & Clean

  • Objective: Strip via patterning resist without chemically altering the exposed 5 nm SiNx active area.
  • Equipment / Tool: Wet Solvent Bench + Mild Downstream O2 Asher.
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters:
    • Solvent strip in Acetone (ambient) followed by IPA rinse (avoid prolonged aggressive oxidizing piranha to protect TiN and thin SiNx).
    • Quick O2 descum in barrel asher: 30 W for 30 s.

Module 6: Lithography 3 – Top Electrode (TE) Lift-Off Patterning

Step 6.1: Dehydration Bake

  • Equipment / Tool: Vacuum Hotplate.
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters: 110 °C for 2 min, chill plate for 30 s.

Step 6.2: Resist Coating (AZ 2070 Negative Photoresist)

  • Equipment / Tool: Automated Spin Coater.
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters:
    • Dispense AZ 2070; spin at 3000–4000 rpm for 45 s (thickness ~6.5–7.0 µm).
    • Automated backside rinse & EBR.

Step 6.3: Soft Bake

  • Equipment / Tool: Precision Vacuum Contact Hotplate.
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters: 110 °C for 60 s; cool down on chill plate.

Step 6.4: Photolithographic Alignment & Exposure (Mask 03)

  • Objective: Align top electrode tracks perpendicularly across bottom lines (forming crossbar nanodevices) and cover open bottom pad.
  • Equipment / Tool: Mask Aligner (Süss MA6).
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters:
    • Multi-layer mask alignment to Mask 01 fiducials.
    • Mode: Vacuum contact.
    • i-line exposure dose: 140–180 mJ/cm².

Step 6.5: Post-Exposure Bake (PEB)

  • Equipment / Tool: Precision Vacuum Contact Hotplate.
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters: 110 °C for 60 s; chill plate for 30 s.

Step 6.6: Development & Descum

  • Equipment / Tool: Wet Bench & Downstream Asher.
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters:
    • Immersion/puddle development in AZ 726 MIF for 65 s.
    • DI cascade rinse > 60 s, N2 dry.
    • Mild O2 plasma descum: 30 W, 50 mTorr, 20 s (removes scum from junction window without punching through the 5 nm SiNx barrier).

Module 7: Top Electrode Metallisation & Final Device Release

Step 7.1: Top TiN Sputtering (60 nm)

  • Objective: Deposit pristine top electrode layer directly capping the active 5 nm SiNx barrier.
  • Equipment / Tool: DC Magnetron Sputter System.
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters:
    • Target: Pure Ti (99.995%) reactive sputter with N2 (identical baseline to Step 3.2).
    • Base Pressure: < 5 × 10⁻⁷ Torr.
    • Working Pressure: 3.0 mTorr (Ar = 20 sccm, N2 = 4–6 sccm).
    • DC Sputter Power: 250 W (keep substrate near ambient to prevent dielectric breakdown or elemental interdiffusion).
    • Deposition Time: ~400 s to reach 60 nm TiN.

Step 7.2: Final Lift-Off & Resist Removal

  • Objective: Dissolve AZ 2070 to leave behind isolated TE lines and probe bonding pads.
  • Equipment / Tool: Solvent Bench with heated ultrasonic immersion bath.
  • Recipe / Operating Parameters:
    • Heated NMP or TechniStrip NI555 at 75 °C for 45–90 min.
    • Gentle sonication (intermittent 10-second pulses at low power) to assist flake separation.
    • Thorough flush with fresh IPA to prevent redeposition of particulate metal flakes.
    • Cascade DI water rinse.
    • Controlled blow-dry under dry N2.

Module 8: Quality Control & Electrical Metrology

Step 8.1: Physical Inspection

  • Optical Inspection: Check alignment tolerances (BE/Via, BE/TE overlap), confirm edge clearance and absence of metal flagging.
  • Atomic Force Microscopy (AFM): Inspect top electrode roughness (\(R_q < 1.0\text{ nm}\)) and BE-to-SiNx step coverage profile.
  • Cross-Sectional Focused Ion Beam / SEM (FIB-SEM): Validate junction layer integrity:
    • \(\text{TiN (BE)} \approx 60\text{ nm}\)
    • \(\text{SiN}_x \approx 5\text{ nm}\)
    • \(\text{TiN (TE)} \approx 60\text{ nm}\)

Step 8.2: Initial Electrical Screening

  • Equipment / Tool: Semi-automated Cryogenic/Ambient Probe Station coupled to a Semiconductor Parameter Analyser (e.g., Keysight B1500A / Keithley 4200A-SCS).
  • Test Sequence:
    1. Low-voltage ohmic continuity verification on BE and TE line monitors.
    2. DC IV sweep (0 V → \(+V_{\text{form}} \to -V_{\text{reset}}\)) with strict compliance current (\(I_{\text{CC}} = 10\text{--}100\ \mu\text{A}\)) to test electroforming and non-volatile resistive switching.
    3. Pulsed potentiation/depression testing to evaluate analog synaptic weight updates for neuromorphic applications.
